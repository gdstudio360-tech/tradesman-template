FAQ

Answer the questions before the customer has to ask.

A short FAQ helps reduce friction and gives the customer confidence to make contact.

Do you provide free quotations?+

Yes. Many smaller jobs can be discussed from photos and a short description. Larger work may require a site visit before a firm quote.

Can I send photos on WhatsApp?+

Yes. Photos of the issue, consumer unit and surrounding area often make the first conversation much quicker.

Do you work with landlords and small businesses?+

Yes. The demo service is positioned for homeowners, landlords and light-commercial customers.

What areas do you cover?+

Ipswich and surrounding Suffolk. For locations outside the core area, send the postcode and ask.
